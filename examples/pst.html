Piece square tables

Naddu's piece square tables (the PeSTO values) as heat maps, from white's side with rank 8 at the top. The tables are fetched from the engine with the pst command, so if you edit them in naddu.js this page follows. Middlegame (mg) and endgame (eg) values are blended by the tapered eval as pieces come off. One colour scale is shared by all twelve tables, red is good for the piece and blue is bad, so the tables can be compared with each other.
