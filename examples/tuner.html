Tuner

A gradient descent tuner for the eval, the piece square tables, material, tempo, shelter, smother and cuddle, all of them in both phases. It fits the eval to the results of 725,000 quiet positions from the Zurichess set, the standard Texel tuning recipe: a sigmoid turns the eval into an expected result, the loss is the mean squared error against the actual result, and Adam steps the weights down the gradient each epoch. Every term is linear in its weights so the gradients are exact. The phase weights and mop up are not tuned, mop up is carried as a constant per position. The positions are parsed and the gradients summed by a pool of workers, each holding a share of the data, and the page never calls the engine after the start except to check itself.

The page carries its own copy of the eval as feature coefficients, so on loading it evaluates 2000 positions both ways and reports whether they match. If a feature is added to naddu.js it must be added here too and that check is how you know. Start from the engine's values to refine them, or from zero to watch the tables grow out of nothing. The result is printed as pst and feature lines to paste into the console page or a script, and being fitted to results it still has to earn its place in a match.
