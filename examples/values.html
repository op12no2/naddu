Effective piece values

The material value of a piece in Naddu is not the whole story, because every piece also gets a piece square table bonus that depends on where it stands, and the tables are not centred on zero. A knight in the endgame, for instance, averages a penalty over the 64 squares, so a knight on a random square is worth less than its nominal 281, while a pawn averages a bonus over the 48 squares it can stand on. Adding the mean of a table to the material gives the effective value, what a piece is worth on average, and this page shows how that changes with the game phase. The phase counts the pieces on the board for both sides, knights and bishops 1 each, rooks 2, queens 4, so 24 is the start position and 0 is kings and pawns only. The blend is linear so the columns in between are just for reading off. Values are fetched from the engine with the pst command, so if you edit them in naddu.js this page follows.
