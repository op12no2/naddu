Parameter walk

A random walk over the search parameters. The parameters and their defaults are fetched with the search command. Each candidate changes one value of the current best by one step, a tenth of its default or at least 1, up or down, and plays a match against the current best on a clock, two workers per game so no hash is shared, random four ply openings, colours alternated, several games at once. If the candidate scores at least the accept percentage it becomes the best, and the walk goes on for ever. The best is kept in this browser so a reload carries on.

This is not a tuner. At a few seconds a game the error bar on a 40 game match is about a hundred Elo, so most accepted changes are noise, and what wins at this speed may not win at a longer one. It is a toy that shows the shape of automated tuning, of which SPSA is the serious version, with none of the maths. Raising the games per candidate and the accept percentage makes it less of a toy.
